Who We Are

AI for the rest of us

For decades, the sharpest data and AI belonged to banks, governments and big corporations — everyone else made do with the leftovers. Career Intelligence Technologies exists to change that. We build a family of AI platforms that put institutional-grade intelligence into everyday hands, turning scattered, overwhelming data into clear, trustworthy insight you can actually use. Our technology is built off of 25+ years in systems engineering, cybersecurity & AI experience — across Australia, New Zealand & the United States.

Across 73 countries & growing.

AI in the service of everyday people

We're a New Zealand company built on one mission: data equality — the same powerful AI that big institutions rely on, put into everyday hands. Every platform we build is one expression of it, starting where the information gap hurts people most. This is what data equality looks like — and we're only getting started.

Private and secure by design

Your data isn't the product. We build strict guardrails, automated threat modelling, and cryptographic privacy directly into every pipeline — Zero Trust security from the ground up, not bolted on after the fact.

The whole picture, in one place

We pull together hundreds of scattered data sources across 73 countries, so you get a single, clear view of what matters to you — instead of the fragments each institution chooses to show.

Real intelligence, not a gimmick

Every platform is built around large language models from the ground up — never bolted on afterwards. That means the guidance you get actually reasons about your situation, rather than matching keywords and calling it AI.

Built to plug in anywhere

Our intelligence is available to partners and platforms via API, so organisations can put the same power to work inside their own products — reaching the people they serve where they already are.
